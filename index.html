A multidisciplinary professional pivoting into the AI space, focusing on modern information systems and intelligent data workflows. Experienced in geospatial analysis and microclimate modeling, now deepening expertise in Generative AI, RAG, and knowledge graphs to build scalable, context-aware solutions.

With a foundation in Geomatics, I specialize in transforming raw geospatial data from sources like satellite imagery and LiDAR into actionable intelligence. My work involves creating precise digital models of the physical world, enabling advanced spatial analysis, environmental monitoring, and urban planning using tools like ArcGIS and QGIS.

My background as a Civil Engineer provides a deep understanding of infrastructure design, project management, and structural analysis. I have hands-on experience in developing 3D models for urban planning and conducting surveys for large-scale airport projects, always focusing on practical, robust, and sustainable solutions for the built environment.
